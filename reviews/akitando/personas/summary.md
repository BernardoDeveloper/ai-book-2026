# Scoped persona check after the Akitando round (2026-10-14)

Five personas read the preface, ch01, ch04, ch08, ch14, ch18, ch19, ch20, the
epilogue and the two new appendices, and answered three questions about the
narrator. Raw files in this folder.

## Credibility (5/5 yes, with a condition)

The history convinces when it is evidence: the 2001 week (the dot-com closing,
the mentor's call into SAP), dated warnings next to the admitted wrong
calendar, the SAP years behind "the model is blind to private rules", the
numbers in "Três bolhas". It convinces less when it works as credentials
("believe me because of my résumé"), which clashes with the book's own
"currículo não é argumento" (ch18). This became EDITORIAL-RULES R21.

## Bragging (mostly no; specific lines fixed)

Flagged by 3-4 readers and trimmed: "Os avisos têm data." (ch18); "mais de
trinta repositórios públicos" (ch14); "o mais visto do meu canal"
(apestudar). Kept: self-deprecating lines (the Nobel joke in ch18) and the
admitted errors, which every reader counted in the narrator's favor.

## Inconsistencies (fixed)

- Career start: preface "fim dos anos 90" vs 1996 in ch18 and apbolhas ->
  "comecei a trabalhar em 1996".
- Years of experience: ch08 "uns 30 anos" -> "de experiência profissional"
  (programming since 1990, working since 1996).
- "Segunda bolha da IA" (ch01, AI history) vs "três bolhas" (apbolhas, the
  narrator's life): ch01 now says both.
- ch01 "Com a IA vai ser igual" vs apbolhas "não sei se vai estourar": ch01
  now "se e quando a bolha estourar".
- Super Mario "ainda precisa de ajuda" (ch08, 7 Sep) vs "pronto" (ch20, end
  of Sep): ch08 now dated.
- The preface repeated ch20's evidence sentence nearly word for word: now a
  short pointer that also names the two curves.
- "Oito meses" vs "um ano de uso diário": ch20 "quase um ano".

## Not changed (taste or the author's call)

The accountant still wants a direct accounting section (the book offers the
reconciliation example in ch20 and the "this week" list); the junior wants
2024-26 junior-hiring data (not in the sources); ch19 remains a manager's
chapter. ch14's cast of names and ch04's Frank Investigator got figures
instead of cuts (see the figures added the same day).
